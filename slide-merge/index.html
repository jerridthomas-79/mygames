How to play

  1. Aim: drag left or right along the bottom of the table.
  2. Flick: swipe up. A faster swipe sends it farther. Swipe at an angle to curve your shot sideways.
  3. Merge: when two of the same item touch, they become the next bigger item.
  4. Combos: merge again quickly for a score multiplier.
  5. Don't cross the line: if an item rests over the red line for too long, the game ends.
  6. Power-ups: 💣 removes an item, ⬆️ upgrades one, 🫨 shakes everything forward, 🔄 swaps your current and next item. You earn a new one every 1,500 points.
  7. Merge two of the biggest item for a huge bonus.
